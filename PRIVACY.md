# Privacy policy — Reader's Calendar

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Reader's Calendar (`com.freedomfighter.readerscalendar`) is published by Pierre Gallaz. It shows, as plain text, the calendars your phone already holds, and lets you add, edit, move and delete events in them. It is free, has no ads, no account and no tracking.

## Data the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or transmit any personal data to its publisher or to anyone else, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting library.

## Network use

None. The app has no network permission. Your events live in the phone's calendar store and are synchronised by the accounts you configured on the phone (Google, Infomaniak, DAVx5, Nextcloud…), with their own privacy terms; the app only reads from and writes to that store.

## Data kept on the phone

The app's own storage holds only your settings (the view it opens on, the calendars you hid, the text size, the colours). Everything else, every event, stays in the phone's calendar store, where the app read it. Android's own backup may copy the settings to the Google account configured on the phone; that is controlled by the phone's backup settings, not by the app.

## Permissions

The app asks only for what its job needs: reading and writing the calendar, to show and edit your events; exact alarms, so that the home-screen widget redraws when an event starts or ends (a quiet alarm that never wakes the phone). It can open a phone number in the dialer, an address in a map and a link in the browser when you tap one in an event, and does nothing with them on its own.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
