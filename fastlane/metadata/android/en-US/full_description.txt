A black-and-white, text-only calendar made for e-ink phones and for anyone who prefers words to colour blocks. No icons, no colours: white on black or black on white, serif, sans or mono, three text sizes. Part of the Reader's family of apps (Reader's Launcher, Tasks, Notes, Books…).

It reads and writes the phone's own calendars, the ones your accounts already sync (Google, Infomaniak kSync, DAVx5, Nextcloud…), so there is no server setup and nothing new to keep in sync.

Views:
• Agenda: the coming days as a list, grouped by day.
• Month: a grid of numbers; a dot marks days with events, today is inverted.
• Week and workdays: a time grid, events as solid blocks so the gaps read as free time, overlapping events side by side, all-day events in a strip above, the present moment as a line. Swipe sideways for the next week, tap an empty slot for a new event at that hour.
• Day: the same grid, one column wide, with the place next to the time.
• Landscape puts the month grid beside the agenda.

Creating an event is the one frequent action, so it is a tap away everywhere: a "+ new event" row at the bottom of every view, a tap on an empty slot, a long press on a day. The form asks the title first, keyboard up, then the day and the times as quick prompts; everything else is already sensible. Events carry repetition, reminder, calendar, location and description. Choose which calendars are shown and the default one for new events.

Two standard home-screen widgets for any launcher: the next event (one line, + for a new one) and today and tomorrow as a list. Other apps can open it for a day, an event or a new event, so it can be the default calendar app.

No network of its own, no account, no tracking. English, French, German, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian. Free software, MIT licence.
